Plots vs. Built Property

Why Invest in a Residential Plot

A plot gives you a clear, undivided title and the freedom to design and build at your own pace — without paying for construction you didn't choose. With DTCP and RERA approvals in place, ownership and resale are also considerably more straightforward.

  • Approved layouts simplify loans, resale and future registration.
  • Flexible sizing from 1200 to 2000 Sq.Ft to match your plan and budget.
  • Ready infrastructure means you can plan construction on your own timeline.
