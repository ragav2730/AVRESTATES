Project Overview

A Layout Planned for Real Living

Vasantham Nagar is AVR Estates' residential plot development in Kamalapuram, close to Koothanallur, Tamil Nadu. The layout is DTCP and RERA approved, with plot sizes ranging from 1200 to 2000 Sq.Ft to suit different family needs and budgets.

Every plot sits along 32-feet black-top roads, with drinking water, electricity, street lighting and layout-wide security already in place — so you can move from purchase to construction with confidence.
